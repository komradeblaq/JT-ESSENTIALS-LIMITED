    🚨 Important Farm Situations

    Some farm problems require immediate attention. Do not rely only on online information when animals are seriously sick or dying.

    🐔 Sudden Poultry Deaths

    Separate affected birds where appropriate, check mortality patterns, water and feed access, housing conditions and contact a qualified veterinarian or animal-health professional promptly.

    🌡️ Severe Illness

    If animals show severe weakness, breathing difficulty, neurological signs or rapid deterioration, seek professional veterinary assistance immediately.

    🐟 Fish Floating / Mass Mortality

    Check water quality, oxygen conditions, stocking density and recent management changes. Seek expert help quickly if deaths are increasing.

    ☠️ Suspected Feed Problem

    Stop using a suspicious batch where appropriate, preserve the sample and seek professional advice before making major treatment decisions.
